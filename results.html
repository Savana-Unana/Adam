Differences Between Genesis 1 And 2

"Let us make humankind in our image, after our likeness."
Genesis 1:26
"Be fertile and increase, fill the earth and master it"
Genesis 1:28
"The eternal God formed a Human from the soil’s humus"

"It is not good for the Human to be alone; I will make a fitting counterpart for him."
Genesis 2:18

Genesis 1 very quickly displays the majestic nature of Adam I. He is created in the image of God, and immediately is given instructions to take over the world. However, Genesis 2 showcases that humanity, specifically Adam II, comes from the Earth, and cares for it after being told to take care of the garden. One should not associate these as separate sides of humanity. In fact, they are the same. However, they are the main aspects of humanity. Adam I displays the majestic and dignified parts of being human, while Adam II displays the covenantal and redemptive qualities of humanity.

Definitions Of The Four Terms

Majestic

The creative, organizing, striving part of being human. If you got this, then that means you are a super creative person, who loves to focus on expressing themselves in some way, shape, or form. You do tend to procrastinate, however, when you get to do something you find fun, you lock in on it.

Dignified

The serious, disciplened, and self-focused part of being human. If you got this, then that means you are a person who will get things done. You do not care how tedious, or simple the task is. You will set aside time and will accomplish it then. However, you typically will have no free time as you will attempt to waste your free time doing work. And if you don't, you are being a loner.

Covenantal

The friendly, obedient, and commited to God part of being human. If you got this, then that means you are a person who is typically social. You are a joy to be around, and you are able to talk to most people. However, many of the people you are close with are nothing like you in that regard, and you are most likely very religious of a person. You will also not always focus on yourself, and are a people-pleaser.

Redeemed

The humble, dependent, and family-based part of being human. If you got this, then that means you are a person that people lean their shoulder on. However, you are also a person that leans on others. You depend on others, the way others depend on you. You work better in a team, where there is a lack of team leader. If there is a team leader, you will probably do everything they say with no regard for what they ask. You are a very fun person to be around, although some might find you weird.

Adam I And Adam II Communities

To be a person that is similar to either Adam I or Adam II also implies that one belongs to either Adam Community I or Adam Community II. To be part of Adam I’s community is to be part of a community of interests. People only work together in the community because they require each other to accomplish different tasks for the community. Adam II’s community is one of faith. To be part of this community, one must be able to listen to commands given, and to share in the same beliefs and traditions shared by the community, no matter what. In shorter words, Adam I’s community puts together a community to achieve their goals of working their own jobs, while Adam II’s community puts together people with goals of working jobs to be a community.

The Loneliness Of The Man Of Faith

The man of faith. Rav Soloveichik’s entire text, and this personality quiz has been built around him. We have discussed the man of faith minimally. But why is the man lonely? It is because he shares almost identical aspects of Adam II in a world/community of Adam I. The man is obedient, only cares for G-d, and typically does the same as his community members do, but that isn’t what the community of Adam I requires. It requires the man to become his own person by himself, but he cannot do that when depending on others to support him. The loneliness is not social isolation, but instead is social disconnection.
